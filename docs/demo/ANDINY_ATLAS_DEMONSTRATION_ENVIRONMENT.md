# Andiny Atlas Demonstration Environment

## Sprint 5.0 — Enterprise Simulation Platform

### Purpose

The Andiny Atlas Demonstration Environment is a realistic enterprise simulation platform used for product demonstrations, customer presentations, executive walkthroughs, investor discussions, training, and enterprise sales engagements.

Unlike a demo account, this environment represents a fully operational organization with departments, employees, investigations, analytics, Trust Ledger activity, and AI-generated executive intelligence.

# Departments

## Customer Support

Responsible for customer complaints, service requests, account inquiries, payment disputes, and escalation management.

## Operations

Manages transaction operations, settlement monitoring, branch coordination, and operational continuity.

## Fraud Investigation

Handles suspected fraud, account compromise, transaction anomalies, identity verification, and financial crime investigations.

## Compliance

Monitors regulatory adherence, AML controls, KYC validation, audit readiness, and reporting obligations.

## Risk Management

Evaluates operational risk, fraud exposure, incident trends, and enterprise risk forecasting.

## Digital Banking

Supports mobile banking, API operations, online banking services, and digital payment channels.

## Finance

Responsible for financial reporting, reconciliation, treasury operations, and executive financial analysis.

## Executive Office

Provides strategic oversight, executive decision support, governance, and board-level reporting.

## IT Operations

Maintains infrastructure, cybersecurity, access control, and technology service continuity.

## Field Services

Supports branch operations, device deployment, merchant services, and on-site operational support.
