# Purpose

This ledger serves as the executive record of all major enterprise milestones completed during Sprint 4.4.

Unlike the Engineering Handbook, which documents technical implementation, and the Commercialization Bible, which defines commercial strategy, this ledger records **the operational progress that connects engineering execution to commercial readiness**.

Transform **Andiny Atlas** from a single-tenant operational application into a **commercially deployable enterprise intelligence platform** capable of supporting multi-organization customers across finance, healthcare, government, real estate, telecommunications, and technology sectors.
